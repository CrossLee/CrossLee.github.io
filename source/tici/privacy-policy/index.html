隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 3 日 · 适用于 1.0.0 (25) 及之后使用相同数据处理方式的版本

4K提词不要求注册或登录。相机画面、录音、稿件、目光校准和视频处理均在用户设备本地完成。App 不上传这些内容，也不收集或提取生物识别人脸数据。

1. 与人脸有关的图像和目光校准

  • 不收集生物识别人脸数据：App 不进行人脸识别、身份验证、人脸特征提取、人脸模板生成或人脸库建立。
  • 仅处理普通 RGB 画面：前置摄像头画面仅在本机作为普通彩色图像用于预览、校准样片和录制。即使设备使用 TrueDepth 摄像头，App 也只使用其普通 RGB 视频画面，不读取或使用深度、点阵、人脸几何或其他生物识别信息。
  • 校准由用户人工盲选：校准时 App 在本机生成三条每条约 4 秒的短片，由用户观看并人工盲选最自然的一条。App 不自动检测人脸，不分析眼神、视线方向、注视点或身份。
  • 仅保存非生物识别设置：校准完成后，只保存标准化提词坐标、距离、镜像和模式，用于还原用户选择的提词位置；不保存人脸模板、眼神特征或校准片。这些设置保留到用户重新校准并覆盖，或卸载 App。

2. 本机处理、存储和保留期限

  • 实时预览：相机预览帧仅在运行内存（RAM）中处理，不作为预览图像持久保存。
  • 校准短片：三条校准短片只写入 App 的临时目录。完成校准、离开校准页、重录或发现文件无效时立即删除；如果异常中断未能即时清理，App 会在下次启动时删除残留临时文件。
  • 正式录制：每次通过基础文件校验的正式录制都会自动保存在 App 沙盒的 Application Support/GazeSpot/RecordingLibrary/v1/records/<recordID>/。同次录制的原片、1080p 效果预览、最终 4K 成片和必要兼容副本由同一条记录关联；该目录不会加入 App 备份。
  • 保留和删除：正式录制不会自动到期。关闭回看、点击“完成”、保存到系统照片或“保留并重拍”都不会删除 App 内记录。只有用户在“录制历史”中选择一条记录并确认删除，或卸载 App，才会移除相应 App 内文件；这不会删除此前保存到系统照片的副本。
  • 画面处理：自然美颜和质感调色均在本机处理。处理模式可先生成不可保存的 1080p 效果预览；用户确认后，App 会重新从原始 4K 文件生成最终 4K，不会把 1080p 预览放大成 4K。失败或未完成的中间文件会被清理，已校验的原片仍归属于同一条本机记录。
  • 异常恢复：App 使用原子记录和部分文件清理机制恢复完整的正式录制；未完成的 .partial 文件不会作为可播放成片显示。
  • 稿件与设置：用户输入的稿件、滚动和拍摄设置保存在设备本地，供下次继续使用。
  • 麦克风：仅在用户主动录制时采集声音。正式录制中的音轨随该条本机录制按相同规则保留；校准样片中的音轨随临时样片一并删除。

3. 整帧美颜与质感调色

自然美颜和质感调色是对整帧 RGB 图像进行的本机固定视觉处理。相关处理不检测、分割、识别、定位或重塑人脸，也不提取人脸关键点、面部几何或身份信息。

4. 保存到系统照片与 iCloud

只有用户主动点击保存时，App 才通过 PhotoKit 的“仅添加”（add-only）权限把所选成片复制到系统照片图库；App 不读取照片图库。照片是否同步到 iCloud，由 iOS、用户的 Apple 账户和系统设置控制，App 不控制该同步。用户可在“照片”App 中管理已保存的副本。

5. 上传、共享、第三方和训练

  • App 不运营用于接收视频、声音、稿件、人脸画面或校准数据的服务器，也不上传这些内容。
  • App 不包含第三方 SDK，不出售或共享个人数据，不投放广告，也不进行分析、跨 App 跟踪或用户画像。
  • App 不把任何内容用于模型训练，也不建立人脸数据库。
  • 第三方接收者：无。
  • 从 App 打开本隐私政策或支持网页时，链接不携带视频、图像、录音、稿件或校准数据。

6. 权限、删除和儿童隐私

用户可以在 iOS“设置”中随时撤销相机、麦克风或照片“仅添加”权限。用户可在“录制历史”中选择一条正式录制并确认删除；覆盖升级应保留本机录制历史，卸载 App 会删除其沙盒内的稿件、设置、校准结果和全部 App 内录制。已经复制到系统照片的成片继续由用户在“照片”App 中管理。

App 不面向儿童建立账户，也不以收集儿童个人数据为目的。

7. 政策更新

如果功能或数据处理方式发生变化，本页面会同步更新日期和内容，并在适用时通过 App Store 版本说明告知用户。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.0 (25) and later versions using the same data practices

4K Teleprompter does not require registration or sign-in. Camera images, audio, scripts, gaze calibration, and video processing remain on the user's device. The app does not upload this content and does not collect or extract biometric face data.

1. Face-Related Images and Gaze Calibration

  • No biometric face data: The app does not perform face recognition, identity verification, facial feature extraction, face-template creation, or face-database creation.
  • Ordinary RGB images only: Front-camera images are handled locally as ordinary color images for preview, calibration clips, and recording. Even on a device with a TrueDepth camera, the app uses only the ordinary RGB video feed. It does not access or use depth data, dot projections, face geometry, or other biometric information.
  • Manual blinded calibration: The app creates three clips of about four seconds each on the device. The user watches them and manually selects the most natural clip without seeing which option produced it. The app does not automatically detect faces or analyze eyes, gaze direction, fixation, or identity.
  • Only non-biometric settings are retained: After calibration, the app stores only normalized teleprompter coordinates, distance, mirror setting, and mode so it can restore the user's chosen teleprompter position. It does not retain face templates, gaze features, or calibration clips. These settings remain until the user recalibrates and replaces them or uninstalls the app.

2. On-Device Processing, Storage, and Retention

  • Live preview: Camera preview frames are processed only in runtime memory (RAM) and are not persistently saved as preview images.
  • Calibration clips: The three calibration clips are written only to the app's temporary directory. They are deleted when calibration finishes, the user leaves calibration, the user records them again, or a file is invalid. If an interruption prevents immediate cleanup, the app removes any remaining temporary clips on the next launch.
  • Formal recordings: Every formal recording that passes basic file validation is automatically stored in the app sandbox at Application Support/GazeSpot/RecordingLibrary/v1/records/<recordID>/. The source video, 1080p effects preview, final 4K video, and any required compatibility copy for one session belong to the same record. This directory is excluded from app backup.
  • Retention and deletion: Formal recordings do not expire automatically. Closing review, tapping Done, saving to system Photos, or choosing Keep and Record Again does not delete the in-app record. Files are removed only when the user selects a record in Recording History and confirms deletion, or uninstalls the app. This does not delete a copy previously saved to system Photos.
  • Image processing: Natural Beauty and Texture Color are processed entirely on the device. An effects mode may first create a non-saveable 1080p preview. After confirmation, the app generates the final 4K video again from the original 4K recording; it never upscales the 1080p preview as 4K. Failed or incomplete intermediate files are removed, while the validated source remains part of the same local record.
  • Recovery after interruption: The app uses atomic records and partial-file cleanup to recover complete formal recordings. Incomplete .partial files are not shown as playable finished videos.
  • Scripts and settings: User-entered scripts and scrolling and recording settings are stored locally for later use.
  • Microphone: Audio is captured only while the user actively records. Audio in a formal recording follows the same local retention rules as that recording; audio in a calibration clip is deleted with the temporary clip.

3. Full-Frame Beauty and Texture Color Processing

Natural Beauty and Texture Color are fixed visual effects applied locally to the entire RGB frame. They do not detect, segment, recognize, locate, or reshape faces, and they do not extract facial landmarks, face geometry, or identity information.

4. Saving to Photos and iCloud

Only when the user taps Save does the app use PhotoKit add-only permission to copy the selected finished video to the system Photos library. The app does not read the Photos library. Whether a saved copy syncs to iCloud is controlled by iOS, the user's Apple Account, and system settings, not by the app. Users manage saved copies in Photos.

5. Uploads, Sharing, Third Parties, and Training

  • The app does not operate a server that receives video, audio, scripts, face images, or calibration data, and it does not upload this content.
  • The app includes no third-party SDKs. It does not sell or share personal data, serve advertising, perform analytics, track users across apps, or build user profiles.
  • The app does not use any content for model training and does not create a face database.
  • Third-party recipients: none.
  • When the app opens this privacy policy or the support webpage, the link carries no video, image, audio, script, or calibration data.

6. Permissions, Deletion, and Children's Privacy

Users can revoke camera, microphone, or Photos add-only permission at any time in iOS Settings. A formal recording can be selected in Recording History and deleted after confirmation. Updating the app should preserve local recording history; uninstalling the app removes scripts, settings, calibration results, and every in-app recording from its sandbox. Finished videos already copied to the system Photos library remain under the user's control in Photos.

The app does not create accounts for children and is not designed to collect children's personal data.

7. Policy Changes

If features or data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date and revised content. When appropriate, changes will also be described in the App Store release notes.
